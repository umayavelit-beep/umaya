Android Chrome Camera Settings

Path to Camera Settings:
  1. Tap Chrome's 3-dot menu (⋮)
  2. Select "Settings"
  3. Tap "Site settings"
  4. Select "Camera"
  5. Find this website in the list
  6. Change from "Block" to "Allow"
Alternative method:

Tap the lock icon (🔒) in Chrome's address bar, then tap "Site settings" → "Camera" → Change to "Allow"
